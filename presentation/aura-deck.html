Problem

Spatial awareness has to be fast, calm, and non-visual.

Primary user

Visually impaired people need immediate obstacle awareness and optional scene understanding without staring at a screen.

Design pressure
  • Obstacle avoidance needs very low latency.
  • Audio descriptions are useful, but constant speech is distracting.
  • Visual UI cannot be the main feedback channel.

Core Idea

We do not need a full 3D reconstruction. We need an actionable projection.

Compression, not simulation

The phone camera gives an image plane. ML Kit places detected objects in that plane. The vest supplies real distance per body zone. Aura combines them into: what, where left-center-right, and how close.

Presentation line: "We turn a messy 3D scene into a compact 2.5D signal the user can feel."

Projection Layer

The 2D image is split into body-aligned navigation zones.

Left
Center
Right

zone = bbox.centerX < 0.33w ? left : bbox.centerX > 0.66w ? right : center

This makes object positions match the body: left vibration for left-side risk, center vibration for forward path, right vibration for right-side risk.

2.5D Fusion

The fusion step decides the strongest useful signal per zone.

1Detect objects in the camera image.
2Classify each object as left, center, or right.
3Read vest depth in the matching zone.
4Send the strongest non-confusing haptic command.

Example

A person appears in the center of the camera frame. If center depth is near, Aura sends a strong center warning. If the vest already reports immediate danger, Aura avoids duplicate conflicting feedback.

Result

The user receives one clear tactile message per zone: static, dynamic, danger, or clear. The phone can still speak object details on request.

Haptic Language

Feedback is encoded as location, intensity, and pattern.

SignalUser meaningMotor behavior
ZoneWhere the risk isLeft, center, right, or both center motors
IntensityHow urgent it feelsPWM strength, capped for motor safety
PatternWhat kind of object or eventStatic, heartbeat/dynamic, continuous danger

The body reads vibration faster than a sentence.

That is why Aura keeps obstacle avoidance haptic and leaves detailed speech as an intentional action.

Speech On Demand

The watch turns object detection into an intentional scene description.

User action

Press the watch button or shake the wrist. The watch vibrates briefly to confirm the trigger.

Phone response

The Android app speaks the latest scene: grouped by left, center, right, and enriched by proximity warnings.

"Person center. Chair left. Obstacle very close on right."

Why not always speak? Audio is powerful, but haptics preserve attention and keep the environment audible.

Impact

Aura makes spatial information ambient instead of demanding attention.

What is novel here?

The system treats 3D perception as an interface problem. Aura is a spatial translator: it turns the world in front of the user into a tactile map they can trust.

FastVest haptics respond without waiting for speech or UI.
